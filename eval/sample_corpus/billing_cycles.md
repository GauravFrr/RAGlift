# Subscription Billing Cycles & Prorated Upgrades

Subscriptions are billed in advance on a monthly or annual recurring cycle.

Prorated Adjustments:
- Plan Upgrades: When upgrading plans mid-billing-cycle, your account is charged a prorated amount for the remaining days in the period.
- Plan Downgrades: Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle, leaving current plan access active until the cycle ends.

# Billing & Payment Error Reference

Error codes associated with payment processing and subscription billing:

- `ERR_BILLING_CARD_DECLINED`: Credit card issuer declined transaction.
- `ERR_BILLING_INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS`: Account balance or card limit insufficient.
- `ERR_BILLING_EXPIRED_PAYMENT_METHOD`: Payment method expiration date passed.
- `ERR_BILLING_SUSPENDED_ACCOUNT`: Action blocked due to past-due suspended account status.
